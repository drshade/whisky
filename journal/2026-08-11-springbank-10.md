# Springbank 10 — 2026-08-11

Bought from acedrinks.nl at €69 alongside the Kilkerran 12 — well under the ~€90 I'd
budgeted for it. Opened the same week it arrived. This was the bottle I'd flagged as
"try first" because of its reputation for being divisive.

## Palate
- Thick and full-bodied
- Not sweet — savoury instead: umami, soy sauce
- Dark caramel
- Pear and overripe banana
- A white wine note — Sauvignon Blanc, that grassy mineral edge

## Finish
- Long and thick, coats the tongue
- A little anaesthetic on the lingering burn — numbing rather than hot
- Pleasant

## Overall
Very unique and I like it. Not an all-day easy drinker, but genuinely special — the
bottle to reach for when you want something interesting rather than something easy.
The savoury/umami direction is unlike anything else in the collection.

Sits at 88, just above the Peat Monster (87) on sheer interest, below the Bain's 15yr
(89) which had a similar "not casually drinkable but excellent" character.
