# Kilkerran 12 — 2026-08-11

Tasted straight after the Springbank 10 — same night, same region, both 46%, bought
together from acedrinks.nl (€57.99). A deliberate head-to-head.

## Nose
- Sweet, but with a metallic undertone
- Bright
- Floral — rosy and rich
- Caramel flecks
- Apple and pear, but more tart than ripe

## Palate
- A tiny fleck of rotten garbage bin right at the approach — in the vapour just
  before the sip, not in the palate proper. Very welcome.
- Sweet upfront, then dry towards the back
- White wine again — Sauvignon Blanc, same as the Springbank
- Less oily than the Springbank

## Finish
- Very smooth, very little burn
- Numbing again (same as the Springbank)
- Doesn't linger, but leaves some oiliness

## Overall
Tied with the Springbank 10 at 88, and they got there completely differently.
Kilkerran wins on nose and immediate charm — brighter, floral, more inviting.
Springbank wins on body and finish length. Neither is better; they're two answers
to the same question.

Two observations from the pairing:
- The Sauvignon Blanc note appeared independently in both. That reads as a genuine
  Campbeltown regional fingerprint, not a coincidence.
- The metallic/flinty edge is the other shared thread between them.
